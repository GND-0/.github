# Membership

## Who can join

| | Can join the GitHub org? | Can contribute? |
|---|---|---|
| IIIT Dharwad students (any branch, any year) | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| IIIT Dharwad faculty and staff | ✅ Yes, as advisors or mentors | ✅ Yes |
| Alumni | ✅ Yes, via `@GND-0/mentors` | ✅ Yes |
| Students or engineers elsewhere | ❌ Not as members | ✅ Yes! Fork, PR, join Discussions |

Membership doesn't depend on your branch or CGPA. Curiosity and willingness to contribute are what count.

## How to join (≈ 30 minutes)

1. **Set up GitHub properly**
   - Use a profile with your real name (or a recognisable one) and a photo or avatar
   - Turn on **two-factor authentication**: Settings → Password and authentication. *Required.*
   - Optional: add your `@iiitdwd.ac.in` email to your account and apply for the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack)
2. **Read** the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [Community Rules](COMMUNITY_RULES.md)
3. **Introduce yourself** in [Discussions → Introductions](https://github.com/orgs/GND-0/discussions) using the form: your year, branch, interests, and what you want to learn
4. **Request membership:** open an issue in [`GND-0/.github`](https://github.com/GND-0/.github/issues/new/choose) using the **🙋 Membership request** form
5. A Core Team member checks you're an IIIT Dharwad student and **sends an org invite**, normally within 7 days. Accept it from your email or from [github.com/orgs/GND-0/invitation](https://github.com/orgs/GND-0/invitation).
6. **Make your membership public** (optional but encouraged): [github.com/orgs/GND-0/people](https://github.com/orgs/GND-0/people) → your name → *Public*. This shows the GND_0 badge on your profile.

## Levels

Progression is based on contribution, not seniority.

| Level | How you get there | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 🌱 **Member** | Complete onboarding | Read access to all repos, member-only Discussions, events |
| 🔧 **Contributor** | First merged PR in any GND-0 repo | Added to a domain team with triage rights, listed on the website |
| 🛡️ **Maintainer** | See [Becoming a maintainer](GOVERNANCE.md#becoming-a-maintainer) | Merge rights on your repo(s), a say in repo direction |
| ⭐ **Core** | Elected or selected each year | Org stewardship |
| 🎓 **Mentor** | Alumni or industry volunteers | `@GND-0/mentors`, invited to review and give talks |

### Badges (shown on the website and in semester reports)
| Badge | Earned by |
|---|---|
| 🥇 First Commit | First merged PR |
| 📚 Teacher | Writing a tutorial or running a study circle |
| 🔍 Reviewer | 10 PR reviews |
| ✅ Verified | Writing a testbench that found a real bug |
| 🧩 Taped Out | Having a design on a fabricated chip (e.g. a Tiny Tapeout shuttle) |
| 🏆 Hackathon | Placing in a club or external hackathon |

## Staying active

- No activity requirement for **Members**: life and exams happen.
- Org members with **no GitHub activity and no event attendance for 12 months** may be removed during the annual clean-up. You can rejoin any time with a new membership request.
- Graduating? Ask to be moved to `@GND-0/mentors` so you stay connected.

## Leaving

Leave any time from [github.com/orgs/GND-0/people](https://github.com/orgs/GND-0/people) → *Leave*. Your merged contributions stay credited.
