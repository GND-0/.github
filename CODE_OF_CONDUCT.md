# Code of Conduct

GND_0 VLSI Club, IIIT Dharwad

> This code applies to every GND_0 space: GitHub repositories and Discussions, the club website, chat groups, workshops, hackathons, labs, and any event where you represent the club.
> It is modelled on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) and adapted for a student hardware community.

## Our pledge

We want GND_0 to be a place where a first-year student writing their first `always` block and a final-year student debugging a timing violation can both ask questions without fear. We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, caste, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, region, language, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** Everyone was a beginner once. Answer the question that was asked, not the one you wish had been asked.
- **Be respectful of differing views.** Disagree about designs, not about people.
- **Give and accept feedback gracefully.** Code review is about the code. Say *what* and *why*, and suggest a fix.
- **Give credit.** Credit people's ideas, code, diagrams and help, and cite the papers, courses and repositories you learned from.
- **Own your mistakes.** Apologise to the people affected, learn, and move on.
- **Think of the community.** Choose what is best for the group, not only what is best for you.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or unwelcome attention of any kind, online or offline
- Insults, slurs, or derogatory comments, including "jokes", about anyone's identity or background
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Mocking someone for not knowing something ("you don't know what a latch is?")
- Publishing someone's private information (phone number, address, marks, photos) without their permission
- Deliberately derailing discussions, trolling or spamming
- Plagiarism: passing off someone else's work, code or design as your own
- Pressuring anyone to share academic answers, licensed material or confidential information
- Encouraging or helping anyone else to do any of the above

## Scope

This code applies in all club spaces and whenever you represent GND_0 publicly: when you post as the club, speak at an event, or use a club account or email address.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it. You will not get into trouble for reporting in good faith.

| Channel | Use it for |
|---|---|
| ✉️ **gnd0vlsi@iiitdwd.ac.in** | Any concern. Read by the Conduct Committee. |
| 👤 **Any Conduct Committee member, directly** | If you prefer to talk in person (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#conduct-committee)) |
| 🧑‍🏫 **The Faculty Advisor** | If the concern involves a Core Team member or you are not comfortable with student moderators |
| 🚩 **GitHub's "Report content" button** | Spam or abuse on GitHub itself |

Please include what happened, where (links or screenshots help), when, and who was involved. Reports are kept confidential and shared only with the people who need them to act. A committee member who is involved in a report must step aside from handling it.

Matters that fall under IIIT Dharwad's own policies (for example ragging, sexual harassment or other institute disciplinary matters) will be referred to the appropriate institute committee. This code does not replace those processes.

## Enforcement

The Conduct Committee will review every report promptly, normally within **7 days**, and may take any of these steps depending on severity:

| Level | Example | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **Correction** | Rude or dismissive comment | Private note explaining the problem. A public apology may be requested. |
| 2. **Warning** | Repeated incivility | Formal warning, plus a temporary ban from interacting in a specific space |
| 3. **Temporary ban** | Sustained inappropriate behaviour, or a serious single incident | Ban from all club spaces for a set period, and removal from teams |
| 4. **Permanent ban** | Harassment, or a pattern of serious violations | Permanent removal from the org and all club spaces |

Repository maintainers may hide, edit or delete comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and must tell the Conduct Committee when they do.
